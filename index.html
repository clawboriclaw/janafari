Player cards

OVR means overall rating — higher is better, and 99 is the best there is.

The blue pill is his position — like QB, DT or WILL. Open his card and it says the whole word, plus his age, college, abilities and top skills.

The blue pill is his position — PG, SG, SF, PF or C. Open his card and it says the whole word, plus his age, size, build, badges and top skills.

Badges: a red X means he has an X-Factor — a signature power that switches on once he gets hot in a game, and while it's on he's nearly unstoppable at that one thing. A grey ★ means Superstar abilities — always on, a steady boost. On his card, tap any ability name (like "Double Me") to see what it means. The ⭐ Stars filter shows only players with a badge.

Badges: a purple HOF on a card means he has a Hall of Fame badge — the top level of a 2K skill, like Deadeye or Posterizer. Every badge comes in Bronze, Silver, Gold or Hall of Fame. On his card, tap any badge name to see what it does. The ⭐ Stars filter shows only the Hall of Fame players.

End Zone Run

You're Jonathan Taylor, #28. Tap JUMP (or the field, or press Space) to hurdle the defenders. Every 100 yards is a touchdown — arms up, fireworks — and the next drive is against a new team in a new city, a little faster. One tackle and the run is over — then your initials go on the board. Grab a floating football for two seconds of turbo. Seven points a touchdown; the score is on the scoreboard at the top right.

The big button starts a run and then jumps. Pause (P) · Restart · Exit (Esc) · Sound switch (off until you turn it on). Find it under ⋯ More or the 🏈 at the bottom of the page.

Janafari Jam

Two-on-two, one button. Your two Pacers run on their own — you tap to jump, and tap with the ball to shoot. Every basket changes the rules: a beach ball, a bowling ball, moon gravity, giants, big heads, snow. First to 5 wins. Beat a team and the next one comes to town — their two best players, with their real NBA 2K27 ratings. Lose once and your win streak goes on the board.

The big button starts the game, then jumps. Keys: Space or W to jump/shoot · 2 players puts a friend on the ↑ key (or the right half of the court on a touch screen) · Pause (P) · Exit (Esc). Sound is off until you turn it on.
