End Zone Run

You're Jonathan Taylor, #28. Tap JUMP (or the field, or press Space) to hurdle cones and defenders. Every 100 yards is a touchdown — arms up, fireworks — and the next drive is against a new team in a new city, a little faster. A tackle pushes you back 10 yards; the run ends if you get pushed behind your own goal line. Grab a floating football for turbo. Top runs go on the board with your initials.

The big button starts a run and then jumps. Pause (P) · Restart · Exit (Esc) · Sound switch (off until you turn it on). Find it under ⋯ More or the 🏈 at the bottom of the page.
